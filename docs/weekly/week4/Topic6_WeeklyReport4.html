02 · 外部测试 · External tests

换一批题：能迁移，也有副作用New test sets: fine-tuning transfers, with side effects

之前的分数都来自训练用的 SLAKE。这次在 35B 的五个模型上跑了三个英文公开测试集：基座，以及「只做微调」和「掺 300 题」各两个种子。

All earlier scores came from SLAKE, the training set. This week five 35B models — the base, and fine-tuning only and replay 300 with two seeds each — were run on three public English test sets.

03 · 方法 · The ruler

读数之前，先检查尺子准不准Check the ruler before reading it

在 PneumoniaMNIST（624 张儿童胸片）上测「描述里有没有把肺炎片说成正常」时，抽查发现判断规则有漏洞：它靠找「说正常」的措辞，漏掉了 "no apparent abnormalities" 这类说法，而且每个模型漏掉的条数不同，会扭曲模型之间的比较。

Measuring "pneumonia films described as normal" on PneumoniaMNIST (624 paediatric films), a hand check found a hole in the judge: it looked for phrases that claim normality and missed ones like "no apparent abnormalities" — and it missed a different number for each model, which distorts comparisons.

改成反过来问：去掉「没有……」这类否定句之后，描述里还有没有报告任何异常。我人工核对了 50 条儿童片描述和 30 条成人片描述，全部判对。按旧规则得出的「微调后都更能区分肺炎片」这条结论，已经撤回。

The judge now asks the opposite question: once negated phrases are removed, does the description still report an abnormality? A hand check of 50 paediatric and 30 adult descriptions found every one judged correctly. A conclusion drawn with the old judge — that every fine-tuned model separates pneumonia from normal films better — has been withdrawn.

04 · 实验 B3 · Experiment B3

用对齐分数挑预训练数据：这次看不出作用Choosing pre-training data by alignment score: no measurable effect this time

队友（Topic 1）的模型能给「胸片 + 报告」打对齐分。B3 比较两组图文预训练数据：分数最高的 5,000 对，和随机挑的 5,000 对，其余设置与之前的 B2 完全相同，在 3B 上做。

The Topic 1 teammate's model scores how well an X-ray and its report match. B3 compares two sets of image-text pre-training data — the 5,000 highest-scoring pairs and 5,000 random pairs — with everything else as in B2, on the 3B model.

先保证分数可信。他的模型在自己训练过的配对上能认出 17–28%，没见过的只有 1.9%，说明它背过训练数据；所以我从 CheXpert Plus 完整版另抽了 10,000 个它没见过的病人来打分。他的模型在我们环境里复现了验证集分数，用他的方法转的图逐像素一致。

First, trustworthy scores. His model finds the right report for 17–28% of its own training pairs but 1.9% of unseen ones, so it has memorised its training data; I therefore scored 10,000 patients from the full CheXpert Plus that it had never seen. It reproduced his validation score exactly in our environment, and images converted with his code were pixel-identical to his.

预训练本身没帮上主任务，和 B1、B2 一样：看图部分冻结、只有几千条数据，换什么数据都很难在 SFT 之后看出作用。按分数挑也看不出比随机好。这是「没测出效果」，不等于「证明没用」：两组有一半数据相同、打分模型本身弱、SLAKE 对预训练几乎没反应，而且只跑了一个种子。

Pre-training itself did not help the main task, as with B1 and B2: with a frozen vision tower and a few thousand examples, no corpus shows an effect after SFT. Choosing by score did no better than choosing at random. That is an absence of evidence, not evidence of absence: the two sets share half their data, the scorer is weak, SLAKE barely responds to pre-training, and each arm ran once.

05 · 漏报异常 · Missed abnormalities

同一张片子，问和描述是两个答案One film, two answers

队友的测试集有 3,189 张成人正位胸片，每张都有从报告自动提取的 14 类病变标签：2,733 张有异常，231 张正常。每张片子问两次：「有没有异常？只答是或否」，和「描述一下这张片子」。

The teammate's test split has 3,189 adult frontal chest X-rays with 14 finding labels extracted automatically from the reports: 2,733 abnormal, 231 normal. Each film is asked twice: "Are there any abnormal findings? Answer yes or no", and "Describe this chest X-ray".

数据只在服务器的容器里，评测脚本也在那里运行，调用同一台机器上的 35B 服务；病人的图片和报告从没离开服务器。

The data stays in the server's container, where the evaluation runs, calling the 35B service on the same machine; no patient image or report ever left the server.

漏报 · missed
01 / 04

直接问：漏报少了Asked directly: fewer misses

2,733 张异常片里，基座答「没有异常」的有 352 张（12.9%）；四个微调模型只有 55–130 张（2–5%）。

Of 2,733 abnormal films the base answers "no abnormality" for 352 (12.9%); the four fine-tuned runs for 55–130 (2–5%).

02 / 04

代价：误报多了The price: more false alarms

正常片被说成有异常：基座 18%，微调后 41–62%。四次方向完全一致：微调把是非倾向推向「有」，跟 3B 上偏 yes 是同一件事。

Normal films called abnormal: 18% for the base, 41–62% after fine-tuning. All four runs move the same way — the yes/no lean is pushed toward "abnormal", as with the 3B "yes" bias.

03 / 04

让它描述：多数更爱说没事Asked to describe: mostly "all is well"

异常片的描述里一个异常都没提：基座 29%；四个微调模型里三个更差（53–69%），一个更好（15%）。2,733 张下差距依然这么大，说明这是微调后模型本身的不稳定，不是样本少。

Abnormal films described with no abnormality: 29% for the base; three of four fine-tuned runs are worse (53–69%), one better (15%). With 2,733 films the spread cannot be a small-sample effect: it is instability in the fine-tuned models themselves.

04 / 04

同一张片子，两种答案One film, two answers

是非题已经答「有异常」的片子，描述里却说正常：基座 22%，三个微调模型 51–67%（一个 13%）。只看是非题的准确率，会把这个危险藏起来。

Films the model calls abnormal when asked, yet describes as normal: 22% for the base, 51–67% for three fine-tuned runs (one, 13%). A yes/no accuracy figure hides this risk.

标签由 CheXbert 从报告文字自动提取，不是医生看片标注；正常片只有 231 张，误报率是最不精确的数字；每个模型只生成一次。Labels come from CheXbert reading report text, not from radiologists reading films; only 231 normal films, so the false-alarm rate is the least precise number; one decoding per model.

气胸是急症，提到它的描述最多也只有 10.4%（基座 9.7%）。微调后多数病变被点名的比例更低。这里的关键词判定比较宽松（比如出现 fluid 也算提到积液），所以这些比例只会偏高，是上限。

A pneumothorax is an emergency, yet at most 10.4% of descriptions name it (base 9.7%). After fine-tuning most findings are named less often. The keyword match is lenient ("fluid" counts as an effusion), so these rates are upper estimates.

描述很模板化（抽查时看到的）：说正常时几乎是同一句话；说有异常时，反复写「右下肺野有一处阴影」，更像默认说法，而不是真的看了图。

The descriptions are formulaic (seen in the hand check): normal ones are near-identical sentences, and abnormal ones keep placing "an opacity in the lower right lung field" — more like a default phrase than a reading of the film.
