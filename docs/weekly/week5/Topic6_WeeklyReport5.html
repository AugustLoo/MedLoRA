03 · 讨论 · For discussion

加入中文数据Adding Chinese data

苏学长发现：把英文问题换成中文，模型就容易答错。原因是训练数据全是英文，而系统落地后以中文问答为主。

Su found that the models often answer wrongly once an English question is asked in Chinese: all training data is English, yet the deployed system will mainly be used in Chinese.

真实中文题 · real
SLAKE 中文

SLAKE 本身中英双语，之前只用了英文一半：中文测试题 1,033 道，训练题约 4,900 道。

SLAKE is bilingual and only its English half was used: 1,033 Chinese test and about 4,900 training questions.

翻译伪数据 · pseudo
翻译扩充

把 PathVQA、MedQA、VQA-RAD 等英文训练题翻译成中文，作为伪数据补充；用服务器上的开源模型翻译，再人工抽查。

Translate English training items (PathVQA, MedQA, VQA-RAD, …) into Chinese as pseudo-data, using an open model on the server, with a hand check.

计划与想讨论的问题Plan and open questions

  1. 先测：用 SLAKE 的 1,033 道中文测试题考现有模型，量出中文差多少。Measure first: run the current models on the 1,033 SLAKE Chinese test questions.
  2. 再训：在 S2-6datasets 基础上加入中文题，训一个中英双语版。Then train a bilingual version on top of S2-6datasets.
  3. 讨论：翻译伪数据加多少合适？翻译的题能不能拿来考试，还是只用真实中文题考？To discuss: how much pseudo-data to add, and whether translated items may be used for testing or only real Chinese questions.
