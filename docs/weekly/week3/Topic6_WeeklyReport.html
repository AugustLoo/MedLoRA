01 · 问题 · The question

规律会不会只是小模型的毛病Is this just a small-model problem?

前两周所有结论都来自一个 3B 模型。一个自然的质疑是：大模型本来就不会犯这些错，结论不能推广。

Every conclusion so far came from one 3B model. The natural objection: a larger model would not make these mistakes, so the findings may not generalise.

这周老师提供了 Intern-S2-Preview：在 Qwen3.5 上继续预训练出来的 35B 视觉语言模型，混合专家结构，256 个专家每次激活 8 个，实际计算量约 3B。部署在同学的 8 卡 5090 主机上。

This week the instructor provided Intern-S2-Preview, a 35B vision-language model continued-pretrained from Qwen3.5. It is a mixture of experts — 256 experts, 8 active per token, about 3B parameters active — served on a classmate's 8 × RTX 5090 host.

我们重复最能说明问题的两个条件：只做医学微调（回放 0）和掺 300 道三选一题（回放 300），每个都跑两个随机种子。数据文件、提示词、学习率、轮数、批次、LoRA 大小，全部和 3B 一模一样。

We repeated the two conditions that carry the argument — medical fine-tuning only (replay 0) and fine-tuning plus 300 three-class examples (replay 300) — each with two random seeds. Data files, prompts, learning rate, epochs, batch size and LoRA size are identical to 3B.

四个问题：35B 天生会不会说 maybe？短答微调会不会也把它推向 yes？回放能不能修好？代价是不是还只在短答格式上？

Four questions: does the 35B model say "maybe" on its own? Does short-answer fine-tuning push it toward "yes"? Does replay fix it? Is the cost still confined to short-answer format?

02 · 方法 · Method

同一套尺子，接到一个新模型上The same ruler, on a new model

这个模型很新，现成的训练框架都不认识它，有三处不得不和 3B 不同。

The model is new and existing training frameworks do not support it, so three things had to differ from 3B.

接口 API

评估：走同一条路Evaluation: the same path

写了一个 OpenAI 兼容的调用后端，四个评估脚本一行没改，设一个地址就切换。基座和所有训练后的模型，都经同一个 LMDeploy 服务、同一套提示词和打分代码评测。

An OpenAI-compatible client lets the four evaluation scripts run unchanged against a served model. The base and every fine-tuned model go through the same LMDeploy server, prompts and scoring code.

0.054%

训练：自己写脚本Training: our own script

LLaMA-Factory 不支持，改用 transformers + peft，模型按层切到 4 张卡。256 个专家是打包的参数，LoRA 挂不上，只挂注意力层和共享专家：250 层，1,917 万参数（3B 是全部线性层，0.79%）。

Written with transformers + peft, the model split layer-wise over four GPUs. The 256 experts are packed parameters LoRA cannot attach to, so LoRA goes on attention and the shared expert: 250 layers, 19.2M parameters (3B: all linear layers, 0.79%).

关思考 · no thinking

先想后答要关掉Thinking mode switched off

它默认先写一段思考再回答。每题只给 8 到 32 个 token，不关的话回答全是空的。训练和评估都用关思考的模板，3B 本来就不思考，这样才可比。

By default it reasons before answering; with an 8-32 token budget the answer comes back empty. Training and evaluation both disable thinking, which also keeps it comparable with the 3B model.

06 · 代价 · Cost

主任务不掉，通用能力的代价在波动内No main-task cost; the retention cost is within noise

35B 上，掺题对医学题和两个通用探针的影响都小到分不出来。

At 35B, replay's effect on the medical task and the two general probes is too small to separate from noise.

掺题前后，35B 的 SLAKE 封闭题和开放题平均只差 0.1 到 0.2 分。TextVQA 从基座 88.8 降到 86.8 左右，但只做微调的两个种子本身就差 1.3 分，掺题多掉的 0.8 分落在这个范围里。MMBench 基本不动。3B 上「代价只在短答格式」那个结论，在 35B 上变成「代价小到测不出」。

SLAKE closed and open scores move by 0.1-0.2 on average. TextVQA falls from 88.8 at the base to about 86.8, but the two fine-tuning-only seeds already differ by 1.3, so replay's extra 0.8 is inside that range. MMBench is flat. The 3B finding "the cost is confined to short-answer format" becomes, at 35B, "the cost is too small to measure".

07 · 消融 · Ablations

D 组消融：改数据比改超参管用Group D: changing the data beats changing the settings

在 35B 回放 300 的设置上每次只改一样，一共 6 轮；对照、只挂注意力、只练 1 轮这三个又各补了一个种子。

One setting changed at a time on the 35B replay-300 condition, six runs; the control, attention-only and one-epoch runs each got a second seed.

先说一个意外：35B 换种子的波动比 3B 大。同一个设置跑两次，macro-F1 能差 2 到 4 分（3B 约 0.7）。所以 35B 上单次运行差 2 分以内的，都不下结论。

A surprise first: run-to-run spread is larger at 35B. Two runs of one setting differ by 2-4 macro-F1 (3B: about 0.7), so single-run differences under 2 points are not interpreted.

掺题的作用大于任何训练设置。掺不掺题平均差 5 分；改超参里只有「练得不够」可靠地差了 3 分，其余都在波动内。

The data mix matters more than any setting. Replay moves macro-F1 by 5 points on average; among the settings only under-training reliably costs about 3, and the rest are within noise.

唯一可靠变差的是「练得不够」。只练 1 轮，两个种子都比对照低，医学题也低。它是 maybe 说得太多还是太少，两个种子正好相反——只能说练得不够时这一类很不稳定。

Only under-training reliably hurts. Both one-epoch seeds score below both control seeds, on the medical task too. One seed over-predicts "maybe" and the other under-predicts it, so the class is simply unstable when training is short.

共享专家的 LoRA：看不出有用。两个种子平均只比对照低 0.6 分。去掉它，少四分之一参数，测不出损失。

LoRA on the shared expert: no detectable benefit. Attention-only averages 0.6 below the control over two seeds; dropping it saves a quarter of the parameters at no measurable cost.

08 · 新探针 · New probe

让它写一段话：
长段描述没有退化Asking for a paragraph: long-form description does not degrade

之前三个通用能力测试的答案都很短。这周加了第四个：让模型用两三句话描述一张图。13 个 35B 模型全部测过。

The three existing general probes all have short answers. This week adds a fourth: describe an image in two or three sentences. All thirteen 35B models were tested.

日常图片（COCO，300 张）：没有退化。训练后的 12 个模型都写 44 到 52 个词，基座 48 个；没有一条缩成几个字，没有一条拒答；和人工描述的相似度基本持平。短答式微调没有把「写一段话」的能力带坏。

Everyday images (COCO, 300): no degradation. All twelve fine-tuned models write 44-52 words (base 48); none collapses to a few words or refuses, and similarity to human captions is unchanged. Short-answer fine-tuning did not damage paragraph writing.

医学图片（SLAKE，96 张）：有一个初看很强、复查后撤回的发现。第一个种子上，只做微调的模型把 44 张异常图里的 13 张说成了「正常」（基座 4 张），掺题后降到 2 张。但补了种子以后，只做微调的第二次只有 3 张；12 个训练后的模型在 2 到 15 张之间乱跳，掺了题的 rank 8 反而是 15 张，和校准好坏没有关系。这个数主要是随机波动，那条发现撤回。

Medical images (SLAKE, 96): a striking first result, withdrawn on replication. With the first seed, the fine-tuning-only model described 13 of 44 abnormal studies as "normal" (base 4), and replay brought it down to 2. The second seed of the same condition gave 3; the twelve fine-tuned models range from 2 to 15, a replay model at rank 8 scoring 15, with no relation to calibration. The count is mostly noise, and the finding is withdrawn.

它留下一个教训：44 张图的计数太粗，单次运行差 10 张也可能是噪声。要把「漏报异常」当成正式指标，需要更多图、多个种子，最好有人工判读。

The lesson: a count over 44 images is too coarse — a 10-image difference between single runs can be noise. Turning missed abnormalities into a real metric needs more images, several seeds and ideally human reading.

09 · 掺多少 · How much replay

掺多少题：
多少都行，只要别太少How much replay: almost any amount, as long as it is not too little

3B 上画过的剂量曲线（0 / 99 / 300 / 900 道），这次在 35B 上补齐，每一档两个种子。数据文件和 3B 完全相同。

The replay budget curve drawn at 3B (0 / 99 / 300 / 900 examples) is now complete at 35B, two seeds per point, from exactly the same data files.

回放剂量曲线 · replay budget curve
01 / 04

macro-F1：只要掺就有效macro-F1: any replay helps

3B 一路涨到 300 条才饱和。35B 掺 99 条就拿到四分之三的收益，99、300、900 三档之间的差（+1.2、−1.6）比同一档两个种子的差还小。六次掺题的运行全部高于两次不掺。

At 3B the gain keeps growing until 300. At 35B, 99 examples already give three-quarters of the gain, and the gaps between 99, 300 and 900 (+1.2, −1.6) are smaller than the gap between two seeds of one point. All six replay runs beat both zero-replay runs.

02 / 04

99 条：两个规模都把 maybe 说过头99 examples: both scales over-say "maybe"

真实答案里 maybe 只有 55 道。掺 99 条时，3B 说了 120 次，35B 说了 99 次和 118 次，都是两倍左右。掺到 300 条以后，35B 回到 55 到 75 次，接近真实。

Only 55 answers are truly "maybe". With 99 examples, 3B says it 120 times and 35B 99 and 118 times — about double. From 300 on, 35B comes back to 55-75.

03 / 04

说过头的代价：准确率The price of over-saying it: accuracy

35B 掺 99 条，两次准确率都掉到 74.0 和 72.4（不掺是 78.8），300 条回到 77.6 和 78.6。不同的是方向：3B 同时把 yes 答成 no（18 → 36 道）；35B 没有（16 → 17 和 13），它多出来的只是「不确定」，不是「说反」。

With 99 examples, 35B accuracy drops to 74.0 and 72.4 (zero replay: 78.8); at 300 it is back to 77.6 and 78.6. The direction differs: 3B also flips "yes" to "no" (18 → 36), while 35B does not (16 → 17 and 13) — it adds uncertainty, not wrong answers.

04 / 04

通用能力：35B 上哪一档都不掉General ability: no cost at any budget at 35B

3B 的 TextVQA 随掺题量往下走（83.6 → 81.8），900 条还让医学封闭题掉了约 1 分。35B 四档在 86.8 到 87.6 之间，SLAKE 封闭题、MMBench 也都不随掺题量变化。结论：两个规模都推荐 300 条。

At 3B, TextVQA falls with the budget (83.6 → 81.8) and 900 examples also cost about a point of SLAKE. At 35B all four budgets sit between 86.8 and 87.6, and SLAKE closed and MMBench do not move either. Recommendation at both scales: 300 examples.

10 · 思考模式 · Thinking mode

让它先想再答：
想得出不确定，说不出 maybeThinking first: it senses the uncertainty, but still won't say "maybe"

前面所有 35B 结果都是关掉思考测的。一个自然的问题：让模型先推理再作答，它会不会自己就肯说 maybe？如果会，回放就不是必需的。

Every 35B result so far had thinking switched off. The natural question: if the model reasons before answering, does it say "maybe" on its own? If so, replay would not be needed.

做法：用同学的服务直接测基座，打开思考，每题最多 4096 个 token，测考卷 500 题，花了 2 小时 49 分。

Method: the base model on the classmate's service, thinking on, up to 4,096 tokens per question, the held-out 500 — 2 h 49 min in total.

21%

五分之一的题想不完One answer in five never finishes

105 道题推理超过 4096 个 token，还在两个答案之间来回，一个字的答案都没给。真实答案是 maybe 的题，三分之一没想完（yes 题 17%，no 题 24%）。

105 questions ran past 4,096 tokens still going back and forth, with no answer at all. A third of the true-"maybe" questions never finished (yes 17 %, no 24 %).

2842

它其实感觉到了不确定It does sense the uncertainty

真实答案是 maybe 的题，推理长度中位数 2842 个 token；yes 题 984，no 题 1278。模型在推理里明显更纠结，但最后没有把纠结变成 maybe 这个答案。

Median reasoning length is 2,842 tokens on true-"maybe" questions, against 984 on "yes" and 1,278 on "no". The model visibly struggles more, but does not turn that into the answer "maybe".

+1.8 vs +5

答完的题上，思考帮得很少Where it finishes, thinking helps little

在它答完的 395 题上，思考只改了 13 个答案（改对 4 个、改错 5 个），macro-F1 高 1.8；回放 300 在同样这些题上高 4.7 和 5.1。

On the 395 questions it finished, thinking changes 13 answers (4 fixed, 5 broken) and adds 1.8 macro-F1; replay 300 adds 4.7 and 5.1 on the same questions.

思考不能替代回放。模型在推理里「知道」这题拿不准，但它不会把拿不准说出来；掺进三选一题，才教会它说出来。

Thinking does not replace replay. In its reasoning the model "knows" a question is uncertain, but it does not say so; the three-class examples are what teach it to.

— 本节结论 · this section's conclusion
只测了基座一次、一个 token 上限。上限更大会多答完一些题，但就算没答完的 18 道 maybe 题全部答对，也只有 23 / 55，与回放 300 最好的一次（22）持平。回放后的模型开思考没有测。只看答完的题这个比较对思考有利，因为去掉的正是它最难的题。One run of the base model at one token budget. A larger budget would finish more questions, but even if all 18 unfinished "maybe" questions were then right, thinking would reach 23 of 55 — level with the best replay-300 run (22). Replay models with thinking on were not tested. Comparing only finished questions favours thinking, since it drops the questions it found hardest.
